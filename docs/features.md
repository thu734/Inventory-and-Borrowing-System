## Assumptions and policy review

The SRS assumptions A-01–A-07 apply to this whole inventory: a hypothetical single department, synthetic data, pre-provisioned accounts, shared Custodian authority, physical units recorded by people, online browser/database use and a correctly configured server clock. They introduce no additional product features, offline workflow or real university integration.

- **Q-01:** the local SRS marks duplicate rejection resolved; the F-03 contract explicitly records approval, and code/tests support it. Conditional duplicate-rejection wording in that contract has been corrected to match its recorded approval and implemented 409 behavior.
- **Q-02:** the local SRS records retention of competing requests as resolved, consistent with implementation and tests. The plan, System-Level SDS and F-02/F-03/F-04 contracts still record Member 2/team acknowledgement as pending. This inventory does not infer that acknowledgement from code, tests or the local edit; the approval-record conflict remains open.
- **Q-03:** due duration, overdue boundary, timezone and S1-loan backfill remain open for S2.
- **Q-04:** the local SRS settles S1 serviceability/security/HTTP conventions, supported by implementation and SDS. The contracts now distinguish implemented conventions from formal review status; the approval record still requires team reconciliation. Code-editing/retirement rules, query/report semantics and numerical performance targets remain proposed for S2/S3.
- **Q-05:** later deployment prerequisites remain deferred. This synchronization performs no Lab 5 implementation or verification.

The whole-product exclusions and sprint boundaries remain those in the SRS. See the [baseline audit](traceability.md#baseline-audit-and-remaining-inconsistencies) for unresolved document conflicts before final submission.
